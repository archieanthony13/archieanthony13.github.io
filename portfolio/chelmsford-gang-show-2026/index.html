Chelmsford Gang Show 2026

I had the opportunity to light our latest Scouts Gang Show, having never been involved before. I was responsible for the design, programming and operating of the lighting. The venue uses an ETC lighting desk which I was not familiar with beforehand, I had to learn the system from home while designing and programming the show. I learnt a lot during show week such as seeing how a theatre operates as well as how load ins and load outs work on this scale of production.
Well done to everyone involved, I had a blast and hopefully see you all again next year!
