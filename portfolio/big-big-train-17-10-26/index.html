Big Big Train

Thank you to all the band and crew for having me back to help at the final show of the Woodcut tour!
During the day I was helping the crew with the load in and setup. In the evening, I was in the front row for the show which was outstanding and has been filmed for a future release.
Woodcut is one of my favourite albums released this year so I was amazed they played it in it's entirety and it was well received by the audience! Well done to everyone and see you at the next.
