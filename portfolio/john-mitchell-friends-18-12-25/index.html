18th December - John Mitchell & Friends(John Mitchell, Oskar Holldorff, Jez Fielder, Craig Blundell)
I had the honour to do lighting for this one-off concert at Dingwalls.
It was my first time doing lighting outside school and I had to learn how to use the equipment on the day by myself.
Thank you so much to Dave Pickering who gave me the opportunity to come and help and Chris Parkins for promoting the show. Thank you to the band for putting on an incredible gig! I had a great time and hope to do it again!
