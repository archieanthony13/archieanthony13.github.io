Marillion + Haunt The Woods

Thanks to my good friend Yenz Nyholm for allowing me to come in for the day to shadow him on the lighting.
I was allowed to come in to meet the stage crew and see the lighting rig get assembled. I then got to watch the soundcheck from the stage itself which was an amazing view. Afterwards I was at the back of the hall next to Yenz watching him run the lighting.
He allowed me to activate the preset for Haunt The Woods and control the house lights when they came on and off which was amazing
This was my first time seeing Marillion in concert and I really enjoyed it, I'll definitely be coming back!
Again, thank you Yenz and the Marillion crew for giving me this opportunity and I hope to see you again soon
