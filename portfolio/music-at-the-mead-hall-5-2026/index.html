Music At The Mead Hall 5

It was an honour to be back for Clive Nolan music weekend which I had the honour of being able to light this time round with the help of Roger Ashworth who usually does the lighting here.
Great to catch up with friends and see some familiar faces. Well done to all the bands and musicians who performed, it was an incredible weekend!
Once again thank you to Clive, Sian and everyone involved for letting me be a part of this event, sadly I won't be able to make the next one in May (it will be the first one I'll miss) so see you all in September
