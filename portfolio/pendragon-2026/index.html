Pendragon

Day 1
The 1865, Southampton

As I write this I am sitting in my bunk at 2am after just heading to bed. It's been a hectic day but so amazing at the same time.
My day started at about 9:30am, after not much sleep due to excitement, by my parents waking me up so we could leave the AirBnB. They then took me out for breakfast in Southampton so I could have a proper meal before heading to meet the band and crew at the venue for about noon.
Once I arrived I was greeted and grabbed my bunk in the bus. Then we started the load in where I helped Adam on the merch. After about 30 minutes I was roped in to help Clive setup his keyboards so I am familiar with how to do it for some of the shows later on this tour (and possibly Arena in 2 weeks). Then it was straight back to helping Adam sort through all the vinyl, CD, shirts and countless other items we had into a logical system that worked for us.
All of a sudden it was time to grab some dinner before the crush of opening doors. Once doors opened it was full on running the merchandise stand with Adam and we ran it straight through the Twice Bitten set and the interval.
Once the main Pendragon set started, Rachel took me to the side of the stage with Adam (a different one) and Phil so I could watch the show from there and also see what they got up to.
The show was amazing and some of my favourite Pendragon songs were played. After the gig I briefly saw my parents before heading back to merchandise to help the final crush of people and packing away. Once all the merch was packed, I then helped the rest of the crew load the trailer with the remaining flightcases, stools, amps etc.
As it hit about 12:30am it was finally time to wind down in the bus, eat some more food, chat for a bit and then head to bed ready for the bus to start travelling at 4am to Trading Boundaries. So far, I've had an amazing time and this looks to be a great first tour!

Day 2
Trading Boundaries, Sheffield Green

It's 2:30am as I head to my bunk after a busy day and not much sleep last night.
After issues with the bus not starting at 4am due to a bubble in the fuel lines, so eventually leaving Southampton at 7am, I woke up at about 10:30am as we parked at Trading Boundaries. We had a very chill morning as the load-in was at approximately 2pm. Most of this time was spent outside chatting.
Once 2pm rolled around, I helped unload the trailer and I started setting up Clive's keyboards as he had shown me the day before. All was going well until we placed a keyboard on the stand and it started to tip towards us (I think my friend Simon Hill jinxed me by commenting yesterday saying Clive's keyboards are in good hands). After taking it apart twice, we realise one of the legs was upside down within the stand meaning it never clicked into place properly. After all this the keyboards were setup and working and ready for Clive to test my handiwork. All seemed good until he came over to me later and said the sustain pedal was plugged into the wrong keyboard. For a first solo attempt, I'm happy that was the only part that was wrong!
Once Clive was sorted, I briefly helped Dammes mic the drum kit and then helped Adam setup the merch table again, however this time we only brought in the necessities rather than everything as we had sorted it all out yesterday.
I had an hour break before doors opened so I took that opportunity to use the toilet and shower in my parents room at the venue, my last nicety for the foreseeable future.
Doors opened and the merch table was busy. Between me, Adam and Rachel, we took turns to have dinner while the others ran the stall. As it hit 8:45pm, I made my way to the FOH area so I could sit with Alec and Dammes during the show to see what they do, especially as I will mostly be focusing on helping Alec with the lighting tomorrow (technically today now) at Boerderij. In the interval, I was back on merch and then back to FOH for the second half.
After the show I took it upon myself to pack down Clive's keyboard rig as I had not been able to do it last night. This was pretty straightforward and then it was a matter of loading everything into the trailer. Now it's 12:30am and we can head into the bus for some midnight snacks (second dinner) and a wind down while telling stories.
We left Trading Boundaries at about 1:30am but due to some cars in the car park, the bus couldn't get close enough to the trailer so we ended up having to push the trailer to the bus.
Now we are headed for the ferry and I will be woken up at about 6am to go through customs.

Day 3
Poppodium Boerderij, Zoetermeer

It's just gone 4am as I write this, I can't believe I'm still awake due to the tiny amount of sleep I managed to get but here we are
The day started by getting woken up at 5am (after going to bed after 2am) to show our passports to customs and then going back to bed for a couple hours. We were women up around 7:30am to board the ferry as we're not allowed to stay in the bus. Luckily we had access to the VIP lounge which had food (bacon and sausage bap for me) and drinks included. We set off from Dover and after an hour and a half we arrived in Calais where we could board the tour bus.
Once on the bus everybody headed up to bed but I stayed with Rachel and Roger to join the carnet club. The three of us headed into the carnet office and while it was being checked, Roger and Rachel were teaching me about the importance of a carnet! Once this was done, the bus headed for the Netherlands and I tried to catch up on sleep (not very successful)
We arrived at Boerderij at about 2:30pm. It's the first time I've ever been to this venue (and the first venue I've been to abroad) but it's so iconic within prog so I was looking forward to this gig the most and it lived up to the expectation. Once we arrived we started loading in, up the ramp, into the venue. My duty today was to help Alec and setup Clive's keyboards. Clive's setup went well and was straightforward without any issues today (jinx over)!
While I was setting up the keyboards, Alec had already sorted the ground package so I spent a lot of the remaining day with him while he was programming the lighting. At some point, Rachel took me on a tour of the venue to show me the dressing rooms, showers (very important), bar (most important for a post-gig pint).
On the way back from my tour, I ended up helping Adam write out the merchandise labels as I apparently have neater handwriting. Once this was all completed the band started to soundcheck so I took the opportunity to shower before dinner. As the showers are pretty much the other side of the wall from the stage, I could hear very clearly the songs being rehearsed which gave my shower it's own private soundtrack!
Dinner was scheduled for 6:30pm and was absolutely lovely! One of the best dinners I've ever had and the service was incredible! I can see why everybody calls this venue their home away from home. After dinner, I was in charge of ensuring Nick and the bus driver's dinners made their way into the fridge on the bus to be eaten later. I was also in charge of the end-of-show-beer for me, Dammes and Alec as there was no way for any crew to get to us during the show to deliver our beers.
Now it was time for the gig and I was once again stood next to Alec, shadowing what he was doing. This was very interesting as he had his own desk rather than the venue desk like last night. This is why the programming earlier in the day was so important so the show would look good.
The show was excellent and I had a great view, I think this was the best show of the tour so far!
After the show, I spent my time packing away the keyboard rig and helping Alec pack away the ground package. We transported all the gear down the ramp, outside the trailer. Alec was in charge of managing the packing of the trailer and he did an amazing job. We had all of Dammes' gear ontop of what we already had as he is no longer taking his van. This meant everything had to be packed perfectly otherwise there wouldn't be enough room and the weight would be off, preventing us from driving. Hats off to Alec, it all went smoothly and quickly.
After the trailer was packed, there was time for drinks at the venue. We all sat outside having a chat and a laugh and just before 2am (leaving time), we headed into the bus to continue our chats until we all gradually headed to bed.
We have a day off in Paris tomorrow which means we can catch up on some sleep. Rog is planning a culture day to visit some of the art galleries / museums that are open so a group of us are wanting to do that to make the most of the visit. More on that tomorrow!

Day 4
Day off in Paris

Tonight is an early night for me. It's 10pm as I write my tour diary!
Today was a day off in Paris. I wake up just before noon and made my way downstairs to check if Culture Club had already left to go to Central Paris. Lucky for me they hadn't so I quickly had a bite to eat and then we set off. Sadly, Roger was unable to join us so it was just me, Sally, Adam, Aderm and Greg.
We walked about 5 mins to the closest metro stop and 30 minutes later we were at the Louvre. We didnt have time today to go around the museum but we wandered around the glass pyramids.
When I was a child, we used to come to Paris on holiday quite regularly and every time we visited, we went to these black and white pillars. Each time we went it was a challenge to see what the highest pillar I could climb onto was. Having not been to Paris for about 10 years, I had to visit my childhood at these pillars and once again see how high I could get. I managed to climb up the second tallest and my inner child was very happy. Another place my parents would always go to is Willi's Wine Bar. This was also just round the corner from the pillars so I took everyone there just so I could see it again. The bar used to have a piece of artwork created every year by one of the staff. At home, we have prints of some of the artwork so it is quite iconic in my family.
After I had taken everyone on a brief tour of my childhood we roamed the streets looking for somewhere to eat lunch. The place we found was lovely and the food was perfect!
We then decided to walk to Notre Dame because we all wanted to see it and I don't think id ever seen it before. It was beautiful and we took the only group photo of the day next to it. After visiting Notre Dame we went to a bar for a round of drinks and for Adam to buy some fridge magnets before heading back to the bus.
Once on the bus it was very chill just chatting and people sorting out their own food. We are now on our way to Barcelona for the next gig!

Day 5
Sala Apolo 2, Barcelona

Today it's 1:30am as I write my tour diary as we have an early start in the morning
I woke up at about mid-day, having missed everyone going on morning walks. I was able to go for a wander with Greg while talking about all things music around the streets and briefly down the harbour when we bumped into Sal and Jo who were on their way to a cafe. I, who hadnt had anything to eat yet, joined them for breakfast / lunch which was lovely.
We managed to organise the load in for 2:30pm but we weren't allowed to put anything on the stage until 3pm. It was a hectic load in as we had to do it across the road while also keeping an eye out on all the gear to make sure nothing got stolen. As we couldn't put anything on the stage, we unbound what we could and set it up on the floor.
Once we had setup, I took the opportunity to have a shower and they were soundchecking when I came out. I then left with Aderm to have dinner before we came back for doors and I once again helped on the merchandise as it was chaotic and quite spread out. We were busy through the support and even into the beginning of the Pendragon set. My initial plan was to stand with Alec and Dammes but I was penned in at merch so stayed there. Luckily it was a great side-on view so I couldn't really complain.
After the show, which finished at 10pm, we only had till 11:30 until the venue was locked. After a very stressful and fast pack down and load out we had the trailer shut and locked after 51 minutes which was very impressive, again this had to be done across the road. This meant there was time for crew showers before the venue was closed.
Some more food and beer on the bus and stories and jokes being told all round along with me trying to (unsuccessfully) worm my way onto next years tour!
Now, it's off to bed as we are arriving at the venue for about 9am and need to be off the bus as soon as we can as it cannot park at the venue. I must remember to take everything I need such as shower stuff!

Day 6
La Sala, Madrid

Tonight it's 2:30am as I head to bed, ready to catch up on some sleep for the day off tomorrow
Today was a drop-and-go as the bus was unable to park outside the venue. This was to be between 9am - 10am. I woke up at 9:30am and couldn't hear any noise. Worried I may have been left I quickly got up as Rachel came to make sure I was awake, phew!
The load in was down stairs and there was a lift for heavy equipment. We were able to load into the venue quite early, er just had no power until later. I setup Clive's keyboards and Phil then showed me some of the stuff for the drums such as different cymbals and assembling the double bass pedal. We also carried all the empty cases outside the venue onto this metal grid which was a bit wobbly when wheeling a trolley across.
At around 12:30pm, a group of us (most of us) set off into Madrid to find food and explore as we had nothing else to do until power was given to us around 3pm
We found a lovely park which had countless cafes throughout. After going for a wander we picked a cafe for lunch. Half of us chose to have the sharing platter of pulled pork nachos (we didn't share), sadly I haven't got a picture but im sure someone else will post one.
We headed back to the venue for 3pm when there was power so I could test the keyboards to make sure everything works! I then took the opportunity to have a shower as we were limited to 3 per hour as the hot water was on a tank. I then sat with Rachel having an unofficial mid-tour review where I told her how much fun I was having! Then it was time for dinner (thank you Rachel for sending my parents the salad picture) and I helped on merch again when doors opened.
For the actual show I spent my time with Dammes (sound) who was able to briefly show me a few bits during the gig. I thought it would be interesting to see the sound side of the show.
After the gig, we had to pack down fairly quickly again, also considering we had stairs and a lift to contend with and there was a basketball game in the arena so the streets were busy and the bus wasn't allowed near us until 11:30pm. All things considered, we managed to do it quickly enough so the rest of the crew had time for showers.
As has become tradition it was then time for snacks and drinks on the bus until we decide it's getting too late. For me, that seems to be as I feel myself drifting off to sleep. Tomorrow is a day off so no need to wake up early especially as we get to the truck stop at about mid-day.

Day 7
Day off in Oucques La Nouvelle

Tonight is an early night as we have a gig tomorrow so 10:40pm to get enough sleep before load in.
Today I woke up at 1pm after 11 hours sleep. We hadn't yet arrived at the truck stop so I sat up chatting with those that were around. When we arrived around 2pm, me, Rachel and Aderm set off into the village to try and find somewhere to eat this evening for dinner. After our stroll and stocking upon toilet roll we headed back to the bus at about 3pm. Then it was a lot of sitting around as there was nothing else to do. I went for a shower around 5pm and we all headed off for dinner at 6pm at Le Holy Snack. We were told food wasn't available until 7pm so we waited while having drinks and then food arrived. Most of us had burgers, I was hoping for pizza but it was off the menu today! Then back to the bus for more chat and eventually off to bed, ready for Belgium tomorrow.

Day 8
Spirit of 66, Verviers

3:20am as I go to bed after Belgian Beers.
Today, Rachel woke me up as the venue was serving pizzas for lunch. We started to load into the venue and once all the equipment was inside, the pizzas were served which scratched the itch from yesterday.
During soundcheck, Clive found a sticky key on his keyboard which required some force to press down, meaning he couldn't play any soft notes. We're still unsure of the reason so will have another look tomorrow but this meant Clive had to do some reprogramming so the soft piano sound could be played on the other keyboard.
I dont have much else to say about today's gig other than it went amazingly and the food (especially the Chinese) was brilliant.
After the show, me, Rog, Greg and Phil, later joined by Alec went to a local pub for Belgian Beers which was a nice end to the day. Now its time for bed and a good rest before the final night of the tour in Luxembourg

Day 9
Opderschmelz, Dudelange

2:20am as I write my tour diary, dreading the early morning ferry crossing.
My day began just before mid-day when I decided I should probably get up. I'm glad I did as we all went into the venue together for a cooked lunch and a staff meeting as this was the only time we would all be together. This turned out to be a mini awards ceremony (not an end-of tour review like Rachel had told me it would be earlier) where everyone gets an award based on what has happened this tour. I won my award for getting stuck in and helping out with a bit of everything. My prize will be posted tomorrow...
After the lunch, me, Dammes and Alec had another crack at Clive's keyboards and this time we took it completely apart to try and find anything that could fix the sticky key. We found nothing but after reassembling the keyboard, the problem seemed to have vanished. We got Clive to come and test it and he was happy. Once this was done, we unloaded the trailer and began setting up. This took the usual amount of time and then we went straight into soundcheck.
Aderm called me over just as dinner was to be served to cover for him on the merchandise desk as punters were already being let in and he didnt want to leave it stranded while he had dinner. Only a few sales were made before he came and relieved me of my duty so I could have dinner. After taking a portion for Nick onto the bus, dinner was BBQ ribs which is one of my favourite foods so I was very happy. I then had a shower as there had not been time earlier, I usually go during soundcheck but wanted to be there as this was the last show of the tour. I then had a brief wander with Rachel before spotting some friends in the crowd who I went to chat with.
Showtime and it was brilliant as always. I personally think this was the best for the lights as the venue was tall enough for it to look effective and Alec did a cracking job. This is the last show of the tour so I enjoyed it as much as I could! End of show beers all round during Masters of Illusion and all of a sudden the show is over and the pack down begins.
We managed to pack everything away within an hour, we had another tight turn around today but there was enough time for remaining crew showers before all getting on the bus for about 12:30am as we set off for the ferry and saying goodbye to Dammes as he was driving straight home.
We all stayed up chatting about how well the tour went, while drinking wine, and that the cruise is the next Pendragon concert. People gradually drift off to bed as I finish off the remainder of Rachel's leftover Chinese from last night. I decided it was finally my turn after washing up as we have a very early ferry crossing and I don't want to be exhausted.

Day 10
Home

Today I woke up at 5am to go through customs. They required us to bring some hand luggage to go through the x-ray machine. Through passport control I was asked where I was going so my tired response was "home". I went back to bed for about an hour before boarding the ferry. On the ferry everyone was shattered, most people slept but I had some breakfast and snacks. We arrived at Dover and I stayed up for Carnet Club with Rachel and Rog. We had a 30 minute wait while they processed the carnet and then we were on the road. Me and Greg were getting dropped off at Clacket Lane services. Hugs and goodbyes all round to those who were awake and many thanks were given from me as I had such a great time on tour! Then it was loading Greg's car and then about an hour home.
Many stories to tell mum and dad along with my pair of socks which was my award from yesterday!
Thank you everybody for making this tour amazing and thank you for letting me come in and help out and for giving me jobs to do. I had so much fun and cannot wait for the cruise (oh yeah, I was invited to crew on Cruise to the Edge!! I've never said yes so quickly)
