Stranger Things: The First Shadow

I watched the matinee performance in the audience and then had the opportunity to shadow the Stage Management department, specifically the pyro plot for the evening performance which was really interesting. I was also helping with the show turnaround between the matinee and evening performance ensuring everything is reset properly and a lot of dodging out the way of the huge set pieces. Being able to see the show and then how it works from backstage was incredible and I'll definitely be coming back to see it again, I highly recommend this production! Thank you to the Stage Management for giving me this opportunity, I hope to see you again soon!
